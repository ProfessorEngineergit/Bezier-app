Rounding edges no longer locks up the part, and a boss rim rounds at the size that used to fail.

**Rounds and bevels**
- Letting go of the arrow on the small grip beside it left the drag running: nothing was built, the preview stayed on top, and nothing on the part could be picked any more. A release now always ends the drag.
- A round that ends exactly on the next edge, like R2 on a boss 2 mm from the side of a block, was refused while the sizes around it worked. It now builds, 0.001 mm short of that edge.
- The thin line where a round meets a flat face is smooth, so there is nothing to round or bevel there. The app now says that, instead of saying the size does not fit.
- A preview that arrives late no longer lands on the finished result.

**Everything else**
- Version 0.2.1 installs this one with **Update now** in Settings, Updates.
