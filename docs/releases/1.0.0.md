Bezier 1.0. The history of a part has its own place, there is a library of standard parts, and a part can be checked before it is printed.

**History**
- The steps of a part sit in a sidebar on the right. Click a step and the part goes back to it, with its settings open; click the last one and everything is back.
- A time-lapse plays the part being built, step by step.

**Parts**
- A library of standard parts: screws, nuts and washers, measured against the ISO tables, with real ISO threads. Put one on a face and Bezier drills the hole and countersink for it.
- Your own parts, from a STEP file and a picture, and parts from your own server.
- Generators: gear, spring, pipe, boss, rib, lip and groove, snap fit with its strain limit, and a fit test for two parts.
- Threads, draft, primitives, copy and paste, a three-point rectangle, and arc radii you can type.

**Checking a part**
- Wall thickness colours the part by how thin it is, with the thinnest wall named. Hover the part to read the value on the scale, as in Simulation.
- Exploded view takes the bodies apart, and puts the view back when it closes.
- Section view cuts along X, Y or Z, and flips.

**Working**
- Several edges round together, and Delete on a round takes it away.
- Shell, Hole and Revolve open their settings at once; Revolve finds the axis the sketch touches.
- A joint stops at its limit.
- Every number can be dragged up and down, the way the rest of the app works.
- Nib leaves when pushed up, also while work is under way, and the rest of that push no longer turns the view.
- 3MF keeps colours, and Export has **Open in slicer**.
- Drawings lay out first-angle views the way the standard does.
- New projects open in the workspace set in Settings.

**Everything else**
- Version 0.2.x installs this one with **Update now** in Settings, Updates.

**Still true**
- The apps are not signed yet, so macOS and Windows ask before they open Bezier the first time. Every file is listed with its SHA-256 sum in the SHA256SUMS files of this release.
