Text you can type straight away, and a sketch you can move by its handle.

**Text**
- The text field has the focus as soon as Text opens, also after the click that sets where the text starts. It starts empty, and Done waits until something is typed.
- The position comes from that click, so the X and Y fields are gone; the bar shows where the text starts.
- The bar fits between the rails in a narrow window, and the rail names no longer sit on top of it.

**Sketches**
- A finished sketch selected in Items carries its own move handle, next to its settings. A drag moves the sketch and everything built on it, and is one undo step.

**Everything else**
- The rail names step aside for the Move and Rotate bar too.
- Version 1.0.0 installs this one with **Update now** in Settings, Updates.
