# The name and the logo

The code in this repository is open source ([`docs/legal/licensing.md`](licensing.md)). The name of
the product and its logo are not part of that. They say "this came from the project",
and that only means something if it is true.

> ⚠️ Not reviewed by a lawyer, and the product's final name is not decided yet. This
> page says what is intended; it does not register anything.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your work is **based on** it, **built with** it or **compatible with** it,
  in plain words.
- Write about it, teach it, review it, and show pictures of it while you do.
- Link to it with its name.
- Pass on the application **unchanged**, under its name.

## What needs another name

- **A changed version that you give to other people.** Fork the code, and call the fork
  something else. Take the logo out.
- **A service you run for other people** from this code, unless it is unchanged and you
  say plainly that it is not the project's own.
- A name or a logo so close to this one that a person would take yours for it.

## Why

A fork that keeps the name looks like the original, and someone using it cannot tell
whose work, whose fixes and whose mistakes they have. The licence lets anyone take the
code; this lets a person know which copy they are running. Other projects handle it the
same way.

If you are unsure, ask. It is rarely a no.
