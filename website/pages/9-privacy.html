Privacy.

This site sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Its fonts and pictures come from this server.

The host keeps the usual server logs (address, time, page) for a short time, for security. Bezier itself sends nothing anywhere: projects stay on your computer, and Community, Collaboration and assistants are off until you switch them on.
