How to connect one

Questions.

Is it free?

Free for people. Companies will pay; the terms come with the first stable release. The licence is on GitHub.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The desktop app and the browser version both work without a network once they are open. Nothing is uploaded.

Which computers?

macOS, Windows and Linux as an app, and any current browser. A laptop is enough.

Is it finished?

No, it is in alpha. It models, sketches, draws, simulates and renders. The 2D constraint solver is still a stand-in, and the app says so on screen. What works and what does not is listed on GitHub.

Can I see the code?

All of it, on GitHub, with every number and every design decision. It is source-available: free for people to use, under the licence in the repository.

What is the beta?

The next version, a little earlier, for people who want to try it and say what breaks. It is not a separate download: you switch to it in the app’s settings.
