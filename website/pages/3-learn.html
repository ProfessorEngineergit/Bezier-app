Your first model in ten minutes.

Bezier explains itself where you are: every command has an article in the app, with its key in the keymap you chose.

The first model

{{icon:sketch}}

1 · Sketch

Press Sketch in the left rail, click the ground plane, draw a rectangle. Type its sizes into the numbers beside it.

{{icon:extrude}}

2 · Extrude

Finish the sketch with Enter, click inside the rectangle, type a height, press Enter. The step is in the history.

{{icon:fillet}}

3 · Round an edge

Click an edge, type a radius, press Enter. Change the height later: the round follows.

{{icon:export}}

4 · Take it out

Export, top right: STEP for other CAD, STL or 3MF to print, PDF or DXF from Drawings.

{{icon:history}}Lessons you can step into

Watch someone model, and at any moment open the model exactly as it was, with its whole history, and carry on yourself.

Record your own and share them in Community. Every lesson is read by a person before others see it.

{{icon:search}}The docs are in the app

Press / and type what you want to do. Articles, settings and projects appear together. Each article shows the steps, the key in your keymap and a picture of where to click.

Coming from another program? Settings, Interaction: navigate and use the keys of the one you know.

Working with an assistant

An assistant such as Claude can read Bezier’s articles, see your model and what is on screen, measure how fast it runs and change settings. It changes your model the way you do, on screen, so you can watch every step and undo it.

{{icon:mcp}}

1 · Add Bezier to your assistant

In Claude Code: claude mcp add bezier -- node …/apps/mcp-server/server/main.ts. Claude Desktop and others: the same command in their MCP settings.

{{icon:settings}}

2 · Let it connect

In Bezier: Settings, MCP & Automation. Switch on Let assistants connect and type the code the assistant gives you.

{{icon:history}}

3 · See what it did

The same page lists every question it asked and every setting it changed, each with Put back.

It cannot open your files, reach your other projects or send anything anywhere. How that is made sure of.
